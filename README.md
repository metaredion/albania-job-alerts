## Overview

**Albania Job Alerts** is an automated job discovery service designed for engineers, IT specialists, and tech professionals in Albania. Twice a day, it delivers clean, deduplicated job digests directly to Telegram — saving hours of manual searching across scattered local boards and noisy global remote listings.

## What It Delivers

- **Verified Local & Albania-Eligible Remote Roles Only:** Surfaces roles physically based in Albania alongside remote positions explicitly open to candidates in Albania — filtering out US-only, EU-resident-only, and unrelated foreign listings.
- **Focused Tech & Entry-to-Mid Coverage:** Tracks over 100 bilingual (English & Albanian) role and skill terms across **Software & Web Development**, **IT & System Administration**, **Cybersecurity**, **Automation & Data**, and **Intern / Junior** tracks.
- **Zero Duplicate Reposts:** Automatically deduplicates cross-posted and repeated openings so every Telegram digest contains only fresh opportunities.
- **Twice-Daily Curated Digests:** Delivered on a predictable morning and evening schedule with direct application links.
